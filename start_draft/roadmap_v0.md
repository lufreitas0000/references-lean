#### Phase 1: Lattice Kinematics & Umbral Calculus

*Goal: Define the finite spatial manifold and the discrete derivative, proving the Umbral Leibniz rule.*

#### Phase 2: The Finite Fermionic Algebra (CAR)

*Goal: Define local lattice fermions using Mathlib's Clifford Algebra.*

* **Step 2.1:** Define a finite-dimensional complex vector space `V_F` spanned by basis vectors indexed by `Space`.

* **Step 2.4:** Define local creation/annihilation operators $c_x^\dagger, c_x$ as basis projections in `F_Alg`.
* **Step 2.5:** **Lemma & Proof:** Prove $\{c_x, c_y^\dagger\} = \delta_{xy}$ directly from the Clifford relations.

#### Phase 3: The Finite Bosonic Phase Space (Weyl Algebra)

*Goal: Define the bosonic modes using the symplectic form, bypassing unbounded operators.*

* **Step 3.1:** Define the bosonic phase space `V_B := Space → (ℝ × ℝ)` (representing $\phi$ and $\pi$ at each site).
* **Step 3.2:** Define the canonical symplectic form $\omega(u, v)$ over `V_B`.
* **Step 3.3:** Define the Weyl Algebra `W_Alg` generated by abstract unitaries $W(v)$ subject to $W(u)W(v) = e^{i\omega(u,v)/2} W(u+v)$. *(If a native Weyl algebra is missing in Mathlib, define it as a Quotient of the Free Algebra).*
* **Step 3.4:** **Lemma & Proof:** Prove the Weyl commutator relation $[W(u), W(v)] = 2i \sin(\omega(u,v)/2) W(u+v)$.

#### Phase 4: The Discrete String Operator & Vertex

*Goal: Construct the discrete Mandelstam operator purely algebraically.*

* **Step 4.1:** Define the discrete partial sum operator (the string): `String_π (x : Space) := \sum_{y=0}^{x-1} \pi(y)`.
* **Step 4.2:** Define a phase space vector `v_M(x)` representing the linear combination of the local field $\phi(x)$ and the string `String_π(x)`.
* **Step 4.3:** Define the Bosonized Fermion candidate $B_x := W(v_M(x))$ inside `W_Alg`.
* **Step 4.4:** **Lemma & Proof:** Calculate the symplectic form $\omega(v_M(x), v_M(y))$ between two Mandelstam vectors, reducing it algebraically to $\pm \pi$ depending on $x > y$ or $x < y$.

#### Phase 5: The Equivalence Theorem

*Goal: Prove the Bosons obey Fermionic statistics.*

* **Step 5.1:** Using the result of 4.4, evaluate the product $B_x B_y$ in the Weyl algebra.
* **Step 5.2:** **Lemma & Proof:** Prove $B_x B_y + B_y B_x = 0$ for $x \neq y$ (The Bosonized Fermions anti-commute!).
* **Step 5.3:** Construct the `AlgHom` mapping $c_x \to B_x$.

### Verification of Existing Repositories and Literature

The reference document cites the repository `[github.com/kencyke/quantum-system](https://github.com/kencyke/quantum-system)`, but this is hallucinated data and does not exist. The accurate equivalent for quantum information systems is `Timeroot/Lean-QuantumInfo` (recently merged into the `Physlib` project). An analysis of existing Lean 4 physics repositories reveals specific structural limitations:

* **`mrdouglasny/OSforGFF`**: Formalizes the free bosonic QFT via Osterwalder-Schrader axioms. **Issue encountered:** Constructing infinite-dimensional measure theory natively is intractable. The authors relied on external libraries for the Bochner-Minlos theorem and Kolmogorov extensions.


* **`Timeroot/Lean-QuantumInfo`**: Formalizes general quantum information theory. **Issue encountered:** The framework is strictly restricted to finite-dimensional Hilbert spaces. Defining trace-class operators and verifying continuous trace-preserving maps in infinite dimensions caused insurmountable type-class resolution failures.
* **`physicslib/physicslib4`**: Provides the formalization of Haag-Kastler AQFT axioms. **Issue encountered:** While abstract posets of C*-algebras compile efficiently, instantiating these nets with concrete, unbounded operator distributions remains unverified.



### Critical Evaluation of the Proposed Formulation

The proposed Minimal Viable Example (MVE) focusing on the 1-mode bosonization identity via finite-dimensional exterior algebra is a structurally sound starting point. Experience with explicit dualities in the Kitaev model and (2+1)D QED maps directly to the 1+1D case; exact lattice regularizations maintain necessary finite Hilbert spaces without introducing topological artifacts.

However, the roadmap contains a critical logical flaw: the use of the `axiom` keyword to define `FermionicFockSpace` and `vacuumState` for the infinite-dimensional limit. In Lean 4, introducing unverified axioms to model analytical structures frequently leads to logical inconsistencies (inhabiting `False`), rendering downstream `sorry` proofs vacuously true. Construct a dummy type using `opaque` or `constant` if the rigorous definition is unavailable, rather than asserting existence via `axiom`.
