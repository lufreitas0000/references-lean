# ADR-0004: Klein factors

## Context
Klein factors are necessary to correctly commute and connect Hilbert space sectors of different fermion numbers (chiralities) in bosonization. We must ensure these factors behave as exact unitary shift operators.

## Decision
We adopt the Miranda-style definition for Klein factors on budget windows (relying on the Haldane completeness theorem, T6). To ensure robustness and correctness without relying on infinite limits, we will cross-check this definition against an explicit algebraic shift construction.

## Consequences
*   Klein factors will rigorously change the fermion number $N$ by $\pm 1$.
*   We ensure exact unitarity relations strictly on bounded budget domains, avoiding infinite-dimensional operator domain pathologies.
