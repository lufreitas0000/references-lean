# ADR-0006: Exponentials

## Context
Operator exponentiation is required for Bogoliubov transformations and correlator machinery. We must avoid infinite series and topological limits (analysis) to keep the formalization strictly algebraic.

## Decision
We will use nilpotent truncated exponentials specifically restricted to bounded budget subspaces. For purely algebraic operator identities and expectation values, we will evaluate exponentials as formal `PowerSeries` or twisted group algebras.

## Consequences
*   Truncated exponentials on budget subspaces guarantee that finite sums are exactly equal to the exponential, making proofs algebraic.
*   Bypasses convergence issues, keeping the project out of `MeasureTheory` or topology.
