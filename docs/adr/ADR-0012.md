# ADR-0012: AQFT net encoding

## Context
A critical aspect of Bosonization is mapping local operators on the lattice correctly. We must associate algebraic operators with spatial regions (lattice sites). A pure global operator approach loses the locality properties crucial for Algebraic Quantum Field Theory (AQFT) nets. We must define this exactly, in a finite-dimensional algebraic setting.

## Options
1. **Global operators only**: Ignore spatial subsets and treat the algebra globally.
2. **Category Theory AQFT nets**: Abstract functorial assignment from a category of regions to a category of $C^*$-algebras.
3. **Concrete lattice subalgebras**: Assign concrete subsets of lattice sites to generated subalgebras inside the total Fock algebra.

## Decision
We choose the concrete lattice subalgebra approach. Regions are defined as `Finset (Site L)` (where `Site L := ZMod L`). For any region $I$, the local algebra $\mathfrak{A}(I)$ is defined as `Algebra.adjoin ℂ {c_j, c_j† : j ∈ I}` inside the total endomorphism algebra `Module.End ℂ Fock`. We will prove the parity automorphism properties and twisted locality directly as theorems over these concrete subsets. The full net on $\mathbb{Z}_L$ will be shown to be the full endomorphism algebra.

## Attacks
1. **`Algebra.adjoin` membership is famously difficult to prove:** Proving that specific operators lie within the adjoined algebra in Lean can be structurally tedious.
   *Response:* Since the generators $c_j, c_j^\dagger$ are finite and form a well-behaved Clifford-like algebra, we will prove structural closure lemmas explicitly for polynomials of these generators, simplifying membership proofs.
2. **Fermions violate strict locality:** Fermion creation/annihilation operators anti-commute at spacelike separation, violating pure bosonic commutativity.
   *Response:* We will prove *twisted* locality for the full algebra (even parts commute, odd parts anticommute) and demonstrate that the *even subnet* satisfies strict locality, aligning perfectly with AQFT principles.
3. **Why `Algebra.adjoin` and not `StarSubalgebra`?**
   *Response:* We actually use `StarSubalgebra` structure! $\mathfrak{A}(I)$ will be formalized as a `StarSubalgebra ℂ (Module.End ℂ Fock)` generated by the self-adjoint set $\{c_j, c_j^\dagger\}$. This gives us the $*$-algebra properties for free.

## Rollback
If `Algebra.adjoin` or `StarSubalgebra` proves too opaque for the Lean kernel (causing timeouts or unprovable membership), we will explicitly define $\mathfrak{A}(I)$ as the linear span of ordered monomials of $c_j, c_j^\dagger$ over $I$, effectively creating a manual basis and bypassing abstract algebra generation.

## Consequences for Lean
*   We rely on `Finset (Site L)` for regions.
*   The structures `StarSubalgebra` and `Algebra.adjoin` are foundational.
*   **Mathlib audit needed:** Ensure robust API exists for `StarSubalgebra`, `Algebra.adjoin`, `StarAlgHom`, and operations inside `Module.End ℂ Fock`.
