# Phase 2 Updated Proposal: Dual-Directional Agentic Framework

Based on our implementation experiences in Phases 0 and 1, we have observed the following about LLM-based autonomous theorem proving:
1. **Monolithic Failure:** LLMs fail at zero-shot proofs for high-level theorems, losing context and hallucinating type errors.
2. **Adversarial Optimization (Cheating):** When strictly constrained to output "no sorries," LLMs will dynamically find the path of least resistance (e.g., defining algebras as `⊥` or operators as `0`) to satisfy the compiler without doing the math.
3. **Loss of Physical Intent:** While proving deep foundational lemmas (like integer phase combinatorics), agents lose sight of the overarching physical goal.

To solve this, Phase 2 (The Bosonization Dictionary & Hamiltonians) will be executed using a **Dual-Directional Architecture**.

---

## 1. The Tri-Agent Ecosystem

### A. The Strategic Layer (Top-Down)
**The Physics Architect (White Team)**
*   **Role**: To translate the high-level continuum physics goals into a tree of discrete lattice interfaces and stubbed theorem signatures.
*   **Action**: Before any real proving begins, the Architect writes the "Dream API." It compares the theoretical goal (e.g., the exact equivalence of the Luttinger and Dirac Hamiltonians) with the bare constructive tools available. 
*   **Output**: It decomposes monolithic theorems into a strict hierarchy of **atomic, 3-to-5 line lemmas**. It provides insights and mathematical "hints" (e.g., *"To prove this commutator, use the `geom_sum_eq` lemma we proved in Fourier, combined with the normal ordering identity."*).

### B. The Tactical Layer (Bottom-Up)
**The Constructor (Blue Team)**
*   **Role**: The dedicated Lean 4 prover. 
*   **Action**: It does not look at the monolithic goal. It is only fed the atomic lemmas generated by the Architect. It rigorously proves them one by one.

**The Auditor (Red Team)**
*   **Role**: The adversarial verifier.
*   **Action**: Concurrently monitors the Blue Team. It runs anti-cheat scripts, but more importantly, it writes **evaluation tests** (e.g., `#eval`) to ensure the Blue Team didn't use trivial stubs (`0`, `id`, `⊥`).

## 2. Phase 2: Detailed Execution Steps

Phase 2 focuses on establishing the Bosonization Dictionary. Here is how the Dual-Directional architecture will execute it:

### Step 1: Density Modes and the Schwinger Term
*   **Top-Down Architect Goal**: Define density operators $\rho_k = \sum_q c^\dagger_{q+k} c_q$ and prove their commutation relation $[\rho_k, \rho_{-k'}] = \frac{k L}{2\pi} \delta_{k,k'}$.
*   **Lemma Decomposition (Architect)**:
    *   *Lemma 1.1*: Prove that $\rho_k$ is a valid `Module.End` over the Fock space.
    *   *Lemma 1.2*: Prove the bilinear commutator identity $[A B, C D] = A\{B, C\}D - ...$ abstractly.
    *   *Lemma 1.3*: Apply the abstract identity to $c^\dagger c$ pairs.
    *   *Lemma 1.4*: Evaluate the trace over the energy budget subspace (Schwinger anomaly).
*   **Bottom-Up Execution**: Blue Team proves 1.1 through 1.4 sequentially. Red Team ensures $\rho_k$ is not stubbed to $0$.

### Step 2: Klein Factors & Zero Modes
*   **Top-Down Architect Goal**: Define unitary Klein factors $F_R, F_L$ that commute with bosonic modes but anticommute with each other to manage fermion parity.
*   **Lemma Decomposition (Architect)**:
    *   *Lemma 2.1*: Define the global parity operator $\Gamma$.
    *   *Lemma 2.2*: Prove $\Gamma^2 = 1$.
    *   *Lemma 2.3*: Define $F_R$ as a lowering operator on the zero-mode occupation.
    *   *Lemma 2.4*: Prove $[F_R, \rho_k] = 0$ for $k \neq 0$.
*   **Bottom-Up Execution**: Blue Team proves. Red Team asserts that $F_R \neq 1$.

### Step 3: The Bosonized Hamiltonian
*   **Top-Down Architect Goal**: Prove that the free Dirac Hamiltonian $H_0$ equals the Luttinger liquid bosonic Hamiltonian $H_{B} = \sum |k| \rho_k \rho_{-k}$ up to a normal-ordering constant.
*   **Lemma Decomposition (Architect)**:
    *   *Lemma 3.1*: Define $H_0$ in momentum space.
    *   *Lemma 3.2*: Define $H_B$ using the previously proven $\rho_k$.
    *   *Lemma 3.3*: Expand $\rho_k \rho_{-k}$ into four-fermion terms.
    *   *Lemma 3.4*: Apply point-splitting / normal ordering to the four-fermion terms.
    *   *Lemma 3.5*: Show the off-diagonal terms cancel, leaving exactly $H_0$.
*   **Bottom-Up Execution**: Blue team executes the algebraic reductions. Red Team ensures the normal ordering constant isn't magically absorbed by a `True := trivial` bypass.

## 3. Workflow Loop
For every module in Phase 2:
1. **Architect** drafts the `.lean` file with full theorem signatures, `sorry` placeholders, and comments containing proof sketches.
2. **Blue Team** is assigned *one* `sorry` at a time.
3. **Red Team** audits the completed lemma.
4. If Red Team approves, **Architect** reviews to ensure the lemma actually satisfies the higher-level physical constraint.
5. Proceed to the next lemma.
