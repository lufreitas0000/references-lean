\chapter{Umbral Calculus Core}
The shift operator $\Shift f(x) = f(x+1)$ and the difference operators $\Diff = \Shift - 1$, $\nabla = 1 - \Shift^{-1}$ provide a discrete calculus on $\Zl$.
We establish the discrete Leibniz rule $\Diff(fg) = (\Diff f)g + (\Shift f)(\Diff g)$ and summation by parts.

\section{Continuum interpretation (not formalized)}
In the continuum limit, $\Diff / a \to \partial_x$. The umbral Heisenberg pair $[\Diff, \beta] = 1$ is the lattice regularized form of the canonical commutation relation $[\partial_x, x] = 1$.
