\chapter{Finite Fourier}
We define the discrete Fourier transform exactly on the finite lattice $\Zl$. Using a primitive root of unity $\zeta$, the plane waves $e_k(x) = \zeta^{kx}$ form an orthogonal basis for functions on the lattice.

\section{Continuum interpretation (not formalized)}
In the continuum limit, the discrete sums over the finite band converge to integrals, and the Kronecker delta $L \delta_{k, k'}$ corresponds to the Dirac delta distribution $\delta(k - k')$.
