\chapter{CAR Representation and Lattice AQFT Net}
We define the Canonical Anticommutation Relations (CAR) algebra and its standard Fock representation on the finite lattice. The local structure is encoded as a net of finite-dimensional $C^*$-algebras $\Net(I)$ associated to subsets $I \subset \Zl$.

\section{Continuum interpretation (not formalized)}
The local algebras $\Net(I)$ correspond to the local algebras of observables in algebraic quantum field theory on $S^1$ or $\mathbb{R}$.
