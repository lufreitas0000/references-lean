\chapter{Lattice, Band, and Index Types}
The spatial lattice is a finite ring of $L$ sites, modeled mathematically as $\Zl$. 
The momentum band consists of modes $n \in \mathbb{Z}$ such that $-L < 2n \le L$. 
This choice avoids division in the natural numbers and perfectly centers the band for even $L$.

\section{Continuum interpretation (not formalized)}
In the continuum limit $L \to \infty$, the spatial lattice $\Zl$ approaches the continuous circle (or the real line if the lattice spacing $a \to 0$ simultaneously). 
The modes $n$ correspond to momenta $k = 2\pi n / L$, which form a dense set on $\mathbb{R}$ as $L \to \infty$.
