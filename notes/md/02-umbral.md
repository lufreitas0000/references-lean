# 1.2 Umbral Calculus Core

## Definitions
- **Shift** $E f(x) = f(x+1)$
- **Forward Difference** $\Delta f(x) = f(x+1) - f(x)$
- **Backward Difference** $\nabla f(x) = f(x) - f(x-1)$
- **Multiplication by x (Umbral)** $\beta f(x) = x f(x-1)$

These form an exact representation of the Heisenberg algebra $[\Delta, \beta] = 1$ on the lattice, replacing unbounded differential operators.
