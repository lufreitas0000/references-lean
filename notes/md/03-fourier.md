# 1.3 Finite Fourier Transform

## Definitions
- Let $\zeta = \exp(2\pi i/L)$ be a primitive $L$-th root of unity.
- Plane waves $e_k(x) = \zeta^{kx}$.

## Properties
- Orthogonality $\sum_{x\in\mathbb{Z}_L} \zeta^{(k-k')x} = L \delta_{kk'}$.
- The forward difference $\Delta$ is diagonalized by plane waves: $\Delta e_k = (\zeta^k - 1) e_k$.
