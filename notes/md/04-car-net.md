# 1.4 & 1.5 CAR Representation and Lattice AQFT Net

## Definitions
- **CAR Representation** $c_j, c_j^\dagger$ satisfying $\{c_i, c_j^\dagger\} = \delta_{ij}$ and $\{c_i, c_j\} = 0$.
- **Fock Space** constructed over the finite index set.
- **Lattice Net** $\mathfrak{A}(I)$ is the $C^*$-algebra generated by $\{c_j, c_j^\dagger\}_{j \in I}$.

## Properties
- Isotony
- Twisted Locality
